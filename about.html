About me

Wearing a few different hats, building in one direction.

Abdullah Al Nayeem Araf is a Computer Science student at Daffodil International University who wears a few different hats — IT expert, independent front-end developer, and Founder of Liberate Minds & DUOR.

Right now, he's steadily building his career toward IT support and infrastructure, while keeping his skills sharp across other areas of tech along the way. Going deep on one path while staying curious about the rest is how his learning stays continuous.

On the front-end side he works independently, shaping ideas into clean, responsive interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.

Beyond the screen, he is also the founder of Liberate Minds, a Digital IT Agency focused on technology, creativity, and digital solutions. Alongside Liberate Minds, he is the founder of DUOR, a clothing brand where he explores his passion for fashion, creativity, and brand building while creating something of his own.

Across all of it, the thread stays the same: technology, creativity, and a habit of continuous learning.
